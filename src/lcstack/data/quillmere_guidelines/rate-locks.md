# Rate locks and extensions

Borrowers may lock their interest rate for 30, 45 or 60 days once a property address is known. A lock that expires before closing can be extended in 7-day increments; each extension costs 0.125 points of the loan amount, paid by the borrower unless the delay was caused by Quillmere.

A borrower may float down once per lock if market rates fall by at least 0.25 percent. Relocking after an expired lock uses the worse of the original rate and the current market rate.
