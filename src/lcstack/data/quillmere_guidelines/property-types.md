# Eligible property types

Eligible properties are single-family homes, townhouses, approved condominiums and two-to-four unit homes where the borrower lives in one unit. Condominium projects must be on Quillmere's approved list, which requires that no single owner holds more than twenty percent of the units.

Manufactured homes, co-operatives, working farms and properties with more than ten acres are not eligible. Properties with active structural damage must be repaired before closing.
