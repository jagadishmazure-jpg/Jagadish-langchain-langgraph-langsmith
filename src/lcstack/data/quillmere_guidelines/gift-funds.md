# Gift funds for the down payment

Gifts are allowed from relatives, domestic partners and fiancés. The donor signs a gift letter stating the amount, the relationship and that no repayment is expected, and the transfer must be documented from the donor's account to the borrower's account or directly to the closing agent.

For a primary residence the whole down payment may come from a gift. For a second home the borrower must contribute at least five percent from their own funds. Gifts are not allowed on investment properties.
