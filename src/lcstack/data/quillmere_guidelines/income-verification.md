# Income verification

Salaried borrowers provide the two most recent pay stubs covering at least thirty days of earnings and the last two years of W-2 forms. The underwriter compares year-to-date earnings on the latest stub with the annualised W-2 figures; a drop of more than ten percent needs a written explanation from the employer.

Self-employed borrowers provide two years of complete personal and business tax returns. Qualifying income is the two-year average of net business income after adding back depreciation. If the most recent year is lower than the prior year, only the most recent year is used.

Bonus and overtime income counts only when it has been received for at least two years and the employer confirms it is likely to continue.
