# Appraisal requirements

Every purchase and cash-out refinance needs a full interior and exterior appraisal by an independent appraiser chosen through the appraisal management company. Rate-and-term refinances with a loan-to-value at or below 70 percent may use a desktop valuation.

When the appraised value comes in below the contract price, the loan-to-value is calculated on the lower appraised value. The borrower can renegotiate the price, bring additional cash to closing, or request a reconsideration of value with three new comparable sales.
