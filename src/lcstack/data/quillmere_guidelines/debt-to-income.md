# Debt-to-income limits

The back-end debt-to-income ratio is total monthly debt payments, including the proposed housing payment, divided by gross monthly qualifying income. Quillmere's standard maximum is 43 percent.

Loans between 43 and 50 percent can be approved only with two compensating factors, such as six months of reserves after closing, a credit score of 740 or higher, or a loan-to-value at or below 75 percent. Nothing above 50 percent is eligible.

Student loans in deferment are counted at one percent of the outstanding balance per month when no payment is reported.
